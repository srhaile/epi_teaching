This tutorial outlines some key statistical methods needed to analyze randomized controlled trials (RCTs), as conducted in our hands-on RCT courses [1], as well as tools in R to do these methods in practice:

- B102 ("Konzepte und Methoden der Epidemiologie") in the Master of Public Health program,
- BME361 ("Randomised trials – From lab experiments to large preventive trials") in the MSc program in Biomedicine, and 
- EPI301 ("Introduction to Epidemiology") in the PhD programs in Epidemiology and Biostatistics; Clinical Science; and Care & Rehabilitation Science.

[1] Radtke, T, V von Wyl, SR Haile, S Rohrmann, A Frei, and MA Puhan. 2024. “Evidence-Based Coaching of Core Competencies in Epidemiology, Using the Framework of Randomized Controlled Trials: The Zurich Approach.” Int J Epidemiol. 53 (3): dyae075. https://doi.org/10.1093/ije/dyae075.
